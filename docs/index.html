Researcher plants 2m canaries into the AI supply chain. A privacy disaster surfaced.

Researcher HD Moore seeded unique canary secrets, including AWS access keys tied to empty accounts, into prompts sent through the chain of AI routers and gateways (brokers such as OpenRouter and Vercel) to see where they leaked. He found evidence that at least three AI providers were compromised by attackers siphoning prompts, and resellers that logged and passed prompts along.

💡 Why it matters: Anything sent through a third-party AI router may be exposed, so keep secrets out of prompts and vet the brokers you route through.
