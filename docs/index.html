Claude Code: idle compaction, auto-mode controls, and retry limits (v2.1.299-v2.1.302)

Claude Code now compacts conversations of 200K+ tokens left idle for about 54 minutes before the one-hour prompt cache expires, and adds CLAUDE_CODE_AUTO_MODE_MODEL, a maximum usage-limit wait for the retry watchdog, and CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_FILE_MENTIONS. 💡 Why it matters: Idle compaction avoids reprocessing huge contexts after a break, which cuts cost and latency for long-running sessions.
